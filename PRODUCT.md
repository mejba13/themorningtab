# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Developers and agency owners who use a Chromium new-tab page to catch up on relevant news and turn it into useful work. The repository implements a personal, bring-your-own-key browser extension.

## Product Purpose
The Morning Tab reads permitted browsing history and pages, then generates a personalized newspaper. Settings control the editorial brief, privacy exclusions, model, rebuild schedule, and optional Social Pulse collection.

## Brand Personality
Editorial, intelligent, composed. The requested settings direction is a premium dark glass editorial desk with sunrise accents and restrained animated details.

## Anti-references
Avoid visual fatigue, loud continuous motion, fake activity, generic dashboard metrics, and sales copy that obscures controls. Preserve existing setting labels and help text verbatim.

## Design Principles
Keep the editorial brief editable. Make the primary action easy to find. Show actual agent activity honestly. Preserve custom settings and privacy controls. Separate immediate preferences from settings applied on rebuild.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use native labelled controls, keyboard-accessible modal navigation, visible focus, readable dark-surface contrast, reduced-motion support, and a single-column mobile layout.
